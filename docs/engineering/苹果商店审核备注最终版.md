# 苹果商店审核备注最终版（PaperTok Reader）

> 用途：可直接复制到 App Store Connect 的 Review Notes / App Review Information。
>
> 说明：以下版本偏稳妥，尽量帮助审核员在没有额外解释的情况下完成主流程验证。

---

## 1. Review Notes（English final draft）

```text
PaperTok Reader is an AI-powered reading and content exploration app for books and visual paper cards.

Primary review path:
1. Launch the app and open the AI tab on the Home screen.
2. Send a text prompt in AI chat, or add image/file attachments to test multimodal chat.
3. Open the Papers tab to browse paper cards with images and details.
4. Open an imported book and test in-reader AI / translation flows.

Important information for App Review:
- The main UI can be browsed without a social account.
- If a provider configuration or review account is required for AI output, please use the review credentials provided in App Review Information.
- Photo access is used for selecting images to analyze or send in AI chat.
- Camera access is used for capturing images for analysis.
- Calendar and Reminders access are only used when the user explicitly triggers those related features.
- The app also supports import / share workflows from iOS share sheet and shortcuts.
```

## 2. 审核员测试路径（建议粘贴到备注或内部记录）

### 路径 A：AI 主流程
1. 打开 App
2. 进入 Home → AI
3. 输入一句文本问题并发送
4. 验证收到 AI 回复
5. 再尝试添加图片或文件附件，验证多模态输入

### 路径 B：PaperTok 内容浏览
1. 打开 App
2. 进入 Home → Papers
3. 浏览卡片流
4. 点开详情页
5. 验证图片查看与收藏功能

### 路径 C：阅读页 AI（可选）
1. 打开已导入书籍
2. 进入阅读页
3. 打开阅读页 AI / 翻译
4. 验证阅读增强功能可用
